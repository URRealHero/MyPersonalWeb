I'm a first-year Ph.D. student in the [VLAA Lab](https://ucsc-vlaa.github.io/) at UC Santa Cruz, advised by [Cihang Xie](https://cihangxie.github.io/) and [Yuyin Zhou](https://yuyinzhou.github.io/). I work on generative models and representation learning, and I'm especially drawn to **world models**. Lately I've been focusing on 4D representations that capture the world's geometry and motion, as a foundation for world modeling.

Before UCSC, I was a research intern with [Zhuang Liu](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/~zhuangl/) at Princeton, and I did my bachelor's at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST) with [Yao Wan](http://wanyao.me/).
